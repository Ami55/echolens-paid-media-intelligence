Paid Media Intelligence

The idea

The starting idea is to connect paid-search intent with a sponsored conversation: a person sees a clearly labeled example ad, opens a separate chat, and explores useful follow-up questions.

The problem it addresses

CSV performance data and ad observations are difficult to connect with messaging and planning decisions.

Who it helps

Marketers developing campaign ideas, creative, and measurement plans.

What you can do

Analyzes Google Ads CSV reports, organizes sponsored-ad observations, explores prompt opportunities, and previews sponsored conversations.

The intended outcome

Turn advertising reports and observations into clearer campaign planning inputs.

Created by SEO Girl - Ami. Review research, generated drafts, and illustrative examples before applying them.
